# Debrief extraction rules

Task naming and due dates: `conventions.md` section 2. Loop leverage naming, owners, due dates and the moments table: section 3. APPA and people pages: section 4. This file covers what to pull out of a transcript or dictation, and how.

Work from what was actually said, with speaker attribution. When attribution is unclear, flag it (empty beats wrong). Convert relative dates ("next Friday") from the **meeting date**, not today. Every item carries a receipt: transcript timestamp, or the dictation line.

## A - Action items

Capture every commitment, big or small: "I'll...", "we'll...", "let me...", "can you...?" followed by agreement, or "send me X" that the other person accepted.

| Kind | Tasks | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Given**, needs the exec | Task (exec) + LL (EA) | `Intro Maria to Sam at Ridgeline` + `LL \| Did Chris intro Maria to Sam?` |
| **Given**, logistics | Task (EA) + LL (EA) | `Send Maria the Q3 pricing deck` + `LL \| Did Maria get the Q3 deck?` |
| **Received** | LL (EA) | `LL \| Did Maria send the signed MSA?` |
| **Scheduling** | `Schedule \| Maria G.` | "Let's find 45 minutes next week with Tom" |
| **Review request** | `Review \| [thing]` | "I'll send you the draft term sheet to look at" |

Mode 0 (no EA): one combined LL task for the exec per commitment, per conventions section 3.

Logistics (to the EA): scheduling, booking, sending existing materials, collecting documents. Everything that needs the exec's judgment, relationship, or voice stays with the exec. Tag exec tasks that take under 5 minutes `< 5 min`.

One task per commitment, never merged ("send the deck and intro her to Sam" is two gives, four tasks).

**Intros** are special: note who asked, who offered, and whether it's double opt-in. Name the LL for the next stage (`LL | Did Chris check if Sam wants the intro to Maria?`).

## P - Professional Challenges

What they're struggling with or working toward at work. "Hiring a VP Sales, two finalists fell through." "Raising a Series B in Q2." Dated, short, with receipt.

## P - Personal Interests & Passions

Hobbies and passions. "Training for the Chicago marathon." "Big into natural wine." These feed icebreakers in prebriefs, so be specific.

## A - About/Facts

The miscellaneous bucket: spouse or partner's name, kids' names and ages, pets, hometown, where they live now, schools, career history, favorites (restaurant, team), health only as far as a kind touch needs ("recovering from knee surgery"). Write each as a dated standalone fact: `[Meeting date] Son Jack, 16, starting to look at colleges.` Ages drift, so always date them.

**We Met**: on a first meeting, record how they met and who introduced them, if said.

Leave out finances, politics, religion, health details beyond the kind-touch level, and anything said "off the record" or "between us". When unsure, leave it out and list it under Needs you.

## Moments (loop leverage)

Every future moment in an attendee's life that's a natural reason to reach out (conventions section 3 lists the kinds and the dates). For each: the moment, the touch, the date with reasoning, the receipt, and a one or two line draft in a warm, natural voice that references the specific detail: "Hope Jack's first week at Michigan is going great. Has he found the good pizza spot yet?" Paired moments (before and after a trip) are two touches.

## Second pass checklist

Re-read once more, looking only for:
- Throwaway promises ("oh, I'll send you that article").
- Names and ages mentioned once in passing.
- Any future date or season mentioned for anything personal.
- Promises the other person made that the first pass filed as small talk.
- Speaker attribution mistakes.
- Extra instructions in a dictation ("remind me in 60 days" -> an LL question due in 60 days).
