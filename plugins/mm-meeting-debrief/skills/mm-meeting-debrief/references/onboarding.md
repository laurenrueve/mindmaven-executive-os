# Meeting debrief: first-run setup and tour

Use this only when `mm-setup` isn't installed. Goal: in about 15 minutes, the user understands what the debrief does, has the tools it needs connected, and has watched it debrief one of their **real** recent meetings. One question at a time. Warm, plain language.

## 1. Welcome

> This skill turns your meetings into follow-through. After any recorded call it pulls out:
> - **Action items**: what you promised, and what others promised you, as tasks in the tool you already use.
> - **People notes**: what you learned about the person, on a running page that builds every time you meet them.
> - **Loop leverages**: upcoming moments in their life worth a touchpoint (a trip, a move, a kid heading to college), each as a task due on the day to reach out, with a draft message inside.
>
> Nothing gets sent to anyone, and you approve every debrief before it goes into your tools. It's one part of the Mindmaven Executive OS; the other skills (prebriefs, calendar review, inbox scans) all build on what this one captures.

## 2. Collect what the debrief needs

Ask one at a time and connect each with `tool-guides.md`, testing with real data:

1. **Recorder**: "Which tool records your meetings?" (Fathom, Granola, Quill, Fireflies, Otter, Zoom, Teams, Meet, other, or none yet). Test: list the last 5 meetings. No recorder: they can paste a transcript or rough notes.
2. **Task tool** and project/list. Teach the task rules from `conventions.md` section 2 briefly. Test: create `Review my first meeting debrief`, due today.
3. **Loop leverage list**: same place as tasks, or their own `Loop Leverages` list. Offer to create it.
4. **EA**: "Do you have an EA or assistant?" If yes, explain Mode 1 from `ea-playbook.md`: logistics tasks and the yes/no loop leverage questions go to them; personal touches stay with you.
5. **Dictation**: "Do you ever dictate notes after a meeting?" If yes, where (voice memo app, Slack audio, email). Teach the APPA order: header, Action items, Professional Challenges, Personal Interests & Passions, About/Facts.
6. **People notes**: existing CRM, or build the Notion People database (`notion-people-database.md`). Explain the running page per person.

Save a partial `MINDMAVEN PREFERENCE SHEET` (`conventions.md` section 1) with these fields, to Claude memory, project instructions, or profile preferences. Show it.

## 3. First real debrief, together

"Which recent meeting should we try?" Show recent meetings and let them pick, ideally with an external person.

Run SKILL.md Steps 1 to 3, narrating each section the first time:
- **Action items**: "Each starts with a verb. Every promise, yours or theirs, also gets a yes/no loop leverage question like 'Did Maria send the MSA?' so someone checks it actually happened."
- **Moments**: "Each becomes a task due on the day to reach out, with a draft message inside. The date is the point."
- **APPA notes**: "Professional Challenges, Personal Interests & Passions, About/Facts. This is what you'd normally forget by next quarter. It goes on their page, with a link back to the moment it was said, so it's there before your next call."

Ask them to edit at least one item. Then push to their tools (Step 4), and open the new person page together, pointing out each section.

## 4. Wrap up

- Next time: "debrief my call with [name]", "debrief today's meetings", send a dictation, "draft the follow-up to [name]", "run my AM Ping", "add a note to [name]'s page: ...".
- Change settings: "update my setup".
- Suggest the next skill to add: `mm-meeting-prebrief`, which reads the pages this one builds.
