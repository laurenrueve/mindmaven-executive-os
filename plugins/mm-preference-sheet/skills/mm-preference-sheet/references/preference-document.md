# Executive Preference Document: structure

Mindmaven's structure, from the Superpowered EA "Executive Preference Document" template and the 2026 "Preference Sheet" template. Keep these headings and this order. There's no such thing as too much information in a preference document: add sections the exec needs.

Each field: `Value  [receipt]` and `(to confirm)` if inferred. Blank fields stay visible as `____`.

```
[EXEC NAME]: EXECUTIVE PREFERENCE DOCUMENT
Last updated [date] | Confirmed with [exec] on [date] | Sources: calendar, email, EA, interview

1. ROLE
- Title, company, what they own, who they report to, key priorities this year
- How I work: link to their "How I Work" doc, or the key points

2. GENERAL
- Company | Exec's name | Email address(es) | Phone number(s) | Primary time zone
- Primary EA: name, email, phone, working hours
- Secondary EA / backup: name, email, phone, working hours

3. WORKING HOURS
- Typical days and hours, lunch break
- Evenings and weekends: what's OK, what isn't

4. COMMUNICATION
- Channels by purpose (e.g. chat for Y/N and FYIs, task tool for requests, video for discussions, email for external only, text for urgent)
- Responsiveness expected, both ways
- How to flag calendar changes (e.g. FYI message for next-day bookings; "(!) FYI" for same-day cancellations)
- Weekly sync: day, time, standing agenda (e.g. calendar review, open questions, drafts to review)

5. EMAIL
- Address(es) and system(s)
- Writing: greetings (and how often each is used), sign-offs (default / when asking / before a meeting), contractions Y/N, words to avoid, paragraph and formatting rules, link style (hyperlinked / raw URL), acronyms (CEO / C.E.O.), other eDraft preferences (e.g. always reference something personal)
- Common types of emails and templates
- Inbox shadowing labels: Ready (draft ready to send) | Pls Read (draft needs the exec) | Pls Handle (exec must handle) | FYI (no action) | Urgent (with a direct message) | optional: Newsletter, Unsubscribe
- Drafting, Pls Handle and FYI preferences

6. MEETING DEBRIEFS
- Contact management tool (CRM or People database)
- Which meetings get debriefed (e.g. all external, all recruiting, no internal)
- AM Ping channel and template (the morning list of meetings with no debrief)

7. SCHEDULING
- Same-day requests: internal vs external rules
- Confirmation emails 24 hours ahead: Y/N, guidelines, template
- Meeting interface in priority order; virtual room links
- Go-to places for coffee / breakfast / lunch / dinner / drinks (name, address, phone, reservations, hours)
- Meeting priority order (e.g. board, clients, prospects, internal, everything else)
- Recurring meetings: name, day/time, length, frequency, **can it move? Y/N**
- Standard meeting lengths and exceptions
- Schedulable hours: earliest start, latest end; days for external meetings
- Lunch
- Calendar event title format (and abbreviations: (C) call, (T)(C) tentative call, (M) in-office, (O) off-site) and event body (both phone numbers, agenda)
- Color coding: exact Google event color names and IDs (Lavender 1, Sage 2, Grape 3, Flamingo 4, Banana 5, Tangerine 6, Peacock 7, Graphite 8, Blueberry 9, Basil 10, Tomato 11) or "calendar color"
- Alerts: when and how the EA updates the exec about calendar changes
- Buffers between meetings; max back-to-back before a break
- Max meetings or meeting hours per day; no-meeting days
- Whitespace: hours per week and where (Mindmaven minimum 4-6)
- Other scheduling preferences (e.g. mornings over afternoons, batch calls together)

8. TRAVEL
- Airlines, seat, class, loyalty numbers (only with the exec's yes)
- Home airport; nonstop preference; red-eyes; arrival lead time; buffer from landing to first meeting
- Hotels and loyalty programs; vacation rentals
- Rideshare / rental car / driver

9. LIFESTYLE
- Diet and allergies (as the exec states them)
- Gifting style (experiences vs things), occasions to remember
- Deep-work rules, do-not-disturb signals
- Pet peeves / triggers
- What the EA should NEVER do

10. INTERVIEW GUIDE  (removed or archived once the interview is done)
- Confirm (Y/N): ...
- Ask: ...
- Next time: ...

CHANGES
- [date] [section] [old] -> [new]  [receipt]
```
